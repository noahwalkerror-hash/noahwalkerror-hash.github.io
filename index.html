Current work

From the Quantum Particle to Nonrelativistic Quantum Fields: A Mathematical Tour

August 2026–present

URECA Project · Nanyang Technological University

Supervisor: Prof. François Gay-Balmaz

Developing a mathematically structured route from single-particle quantum mechanics to nonrelativistic quantum field theory. The project constructs an explicit dictionary between one-particle and second-quantized formulations, including bosonic and fermionic Fock spaces, creation and annihilation operators, quantum fields as operators on Fock space, with optional extensions to symmetry.

Memory Effects in Stochastic Thermodynamics

May 2026–present

Undergraduate Research Project · Nanyang Technological University

Supervisor: Prof. François Gay-Balmaz

Investigating memory effects in stochastic thermodynamics through a variational formulation proposed by Prof. François Gay-Balmaz. Current work applies this framework to the full non-Markovian system, examining fluctuation–dissipation consistency, stationary behavior, and entropy production.
